ABOUT LIBRARY

Learning Resource Centre:

The primary objective of our learning resource centre is to provide information and knowledge to its students and staff through its document collection. Our library has been acquiring different types of forestry and other related documents and organizing them for the efficient usage. It also has a vast collection of reference books; Journals, handbooks, dictionaries and encyclopedia on the subjects to cater to the need and the requirements of the faculty members, researchers and students.

The reading room is well furnished where 300 students accommodate at a time and provides conducive environment for study. Exclusive reference section is available in the library. All educational & research postures and clipping of major events have been displayed. A visitor’s book is maintained for students and staff. New arrivals of books and journals are displayed on separate racks and website.
