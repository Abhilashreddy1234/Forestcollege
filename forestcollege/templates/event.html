Grads sharpen leadership skills with Lake Forest Graduate School of Management

Tech careers are constantly shifting as new innovations move the field forward. Overcoming obstacles and staying relevant can be challenging. How have other women in the field succeeded?

At The Outlet 18: Innovating Your Career in Tech (Wednesday, May 18th from 12:00 – 1:00 PM EDT), our panelists will share their journey, and provide insight into how to be innovative to keep your tech career going strong.

SheTek Founder and President Chaya Pamula will be joined by Dr Shanta Thoutam, Chief Innovation Officer of Telangana State in India. Dr. Thoutam is the first woman to hold this position, where she will be heading the Telangana State Innovation Cell (TSIC). These dynamic women will be discussing their challenges and triumphs they’ve had in their careers, as well as providing insight and inspiration for you in your career journey.

Don’t miss a lively discussion, and the opportunity to get your questions answered by two innovative, encouraging, and inspirational women. Tickets are free! Register today!
